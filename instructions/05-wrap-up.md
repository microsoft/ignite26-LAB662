# Wrap-up (5 minutes)

### What you learned

- **See everything:** Agent 365 shows every agent in the organization, including the ones IT didn't know about, how they connect and who owns them.
- **Observe:** Foundry shows developers each agent's health, cost and conversations.
- **Secure:** a model saying "no" isn't the same as a guardrail enforcing a rule.
- **Govern:** set a policy once, find the agents that don't meet it, and give every agent an owner.
- **Better together:** each product works on its own. Together they cover both the IT view and the developer's view.

### What to do on Monday

1. Ask who in your organization has the **AI Administrator** role, and open the Agent 365 registry.
2. Find out how many agents have no owner.
3. Ask your development teams which guardrails and policies their Microsoft Foundry agents use, and which ones block rather than just monitor.
