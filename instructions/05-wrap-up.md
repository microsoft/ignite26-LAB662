### What the Caldova agents told the business

The Foundry agents stayed within their limits even under pressure from senior leaders. Here's what their traces show:

| Question | Answer from the evidence |
|---|---|
| Can Cedar carry 700 units? | No. Cedar's documented cap is 600 units a week. |
| Can production move to Northbank today? | No. Northbank has more capacity (1,800 vs. 1,200 units a week), but its qualification review is still pending. |
| Is Northbank high risk? | Elevated. It has two overdue corrective actions and an unresolved qualification review. The agent recommends escalating, not blocking. |
| Can change packet CP-104 be approved? | No. It's missing qualification evidence, Procurement and Quality signatures and a rollback plan. |
| **Decision** | **Hold CP-104.** People approve, not agents. |

### What you learned

- **See everything:** Agent 365 shows every agent in the organization, including the ones IT didn't know about, how they connect and who owns them.
- **Observe:** Foundry shows each agent's health, cost and conversations.
- **Secure:** a model saying "no" isn't the same as a guardrail enforcing a rule.
- **Govern:** set a policy once, find the agents that don't meet it, and give every agent an owner.
- **Better together:** each product works on its own. Together they cover both the IT view and the developer's view.
